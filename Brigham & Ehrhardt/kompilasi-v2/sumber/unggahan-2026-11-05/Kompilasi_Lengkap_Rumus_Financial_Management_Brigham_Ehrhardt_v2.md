# **Bab 2: Financial Statements, Cash Flow, and Taxes**

**Cakupan Buku: Halaman 57–100 (Selected Eq: Hal. 1085–1086)**

| **Nama Rumus / Konsep**                                   | **Rumus Matematis**                                                                                                                                                                | **Halaman Buku**     | **Keterangan & Aplikasi**                                                                                                                                                                    |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Earnings Before Interest and Taxes (EBIT)**             | $\text{EBIT} = \text{Sales Revenues} - \text{Operating Costs}$ (termasuk depresiasi & amortisasi) | **Hal. 63, 1085**    | Laba usaha murni dari aktivitas operasi sebelum dikurangi beban bunga utang dan beban pajak penghasilan.                                                                                     |
| **EBITDA**                                                | $\text{EBITDA} = \text{EBIT} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Amortization}$<br>atau $\text{EBITDA} = \text{Sales} - \text{Operating Costs}$ (tanpa depresiasi/amortisasi) | **Hal. 64, 1085**    | Laba kotor operasional sebelum beban non-kas depresiasi, amortisasi, bunga, dan pajak.                                                                                                       |
| **Net Cash Flow**                                         | $\text{Net Cash Flow} = \text{Net Income} + \text{Depreciation and Amortization}$ | **Hal. 70, 1085**    | Proksi kasar arus kas masuk operasional jika penyesuaian modal kerja diabaikan.                                                                                                              |
| **Net Operating Working Capital (NOWC)**                  | $\text{NOWC} = \text{Operating Current Assets} - \text{Operating Current Liabilities}$<br>$\text{NOWC} = (\text{Cash} + \text{Accounts Receivable} + \text{Inventories}) - (\text{Accounts Payable} + \text{Accruals})$ | **Hal. 72–73, 1085** | Modal kerja operasional bersih yang wajib didanai oleh penyedia modal investor (tidak memperhitungkan efek utang berbunga/notes payable maupun kas non-operasional/investasi jangka pendek). |
| **Total Net Operating Capital**                           | $\text{Total Net Operating Capital} = \text{NOWC} + \text{Net Plant and Equipment}$ (Operating Long-Term Assets) | **Hal. 73, 1085**    | Total modal operasional yang terikat secara riil dalam mendukung aktivitas bisnis inti perusahaan.                                                                                           |
| **Total Investor-Supplied Operating Capital**             | $\text{Operating Capital} = \text{Total Investor-Supplied Capital} - \text{Short-Term Investments}$<br>$= (\text{Notes Payable} + \text{Long-Term Bonds} + \text{Preferred Stock} + \text{Common Equity}) - \text{Short-Term Investments}$ | **Hal. 73–74**       | Metode pendanaan (financing perspective) untuk menghitung total modal operasi yang didanai oleh pemegang utang dan ekuitas.                                                                  |
| **Net Operating Profit After Taxes (NOPAT)**              | $\text{NOPAT} = \text{EBIT} \times (1 - \text{Tax Rate})$ | **Hal. 71–72, 1085** | Laba operasional setelah pajak yang akan diperoleh jika perusahaan tidak memiliki utang sama sekali.                                                                                         |
| **Net Investment in Operating Capital**                   | $\text{Net Investment} = \text{Total Net Operating Capital}_t - \text{Total Net Operating Capital}_{t-1}$ | **Hal. 74, 1085**    | Penambahan bersih aset modal operasi selama periode berjalan yang mengurangi kas untuk investor.                                                                                             |
| **Free Cash Flow (FCF) - Operating Definition**           | $\text{FCF} = \text{NOPAT} - \text{Net Investment in Operating Capital}$ | **Hal. 74, 1085**    | Arus kas bebas yang benar-benar tersedia untuk didistribusikan kepada seluruh penanam modal.                                                                                                 |
| **Free Cash Flow (FCF) - CapEx & Working Capital Format** | $\text{FCF} = [\text{EBIT}(1 - T) + \text{Depreciation}] - [\text{Gross Investment in Fixed Assets}] - [\Delta \text{NOWC}]$<br>atau $\text{FCF} = \text{NOPAT} + \text{Depr} - \text{CapEx} - \Delta \text{NOWC}$ | **Hal. 75, 1085**    | Formulasi alternatif FCF yang sering digunakan analis keuangan dari laporan arus kas.                                                                                                        |
| **Uses of Free Cash Flow**                                | $\text{FCF} = \text{After-tax Interest} + \text{Net Debt Repayment} + \text{Dividends} + \text{Net Stock Repurchases} + \text{Net Purchases of ST Investments}$ | **Hal. 75–77**       | Rekonsiliasi 5 alokasi arus kas bebas kepada pemegang utang, pemegang ekuitas, dan investasi non-operasional.                                                                                |
| **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)**                     | $\text{ROIC} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Total Net Operating Capital}}$ | **Hal. 79, 1085**    | Persentase imbal hasil murni dari operasi atas setiap unit modal operasi yang ditanamkan.                                                                                                    |
| **Operating Profitability Ratio (OP)**                    | $\text{OP} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Sales}}$ | **Hal. 79, 1085**    | Efisiensi profitabilitas operasional setelah pajak per dolar penjualan.                                                                                                                      |
| **Capital Requirement Ratio (CR)**                        | $\text{CR} = \frac{\text{Total Net Operating Capital}}{\text{Sales}}$ | **Hal. 79, 1085**    | Rasio intensitas modal operasi yang dibutuhkan untuk menghasilkan satu dolar penjualan.                                                                                                      |
| **Market Value Added (MVA) - Equity Focus**               | $\text{MVA} = \text{Market Value of Stock} - \text{Equity Capital Supplied by Shareholders}$<br>$= (\text{Shares Outstanding} \times \text{Stock Price}) - \text{Total Common Equity}$ | **Hal. 81, 1085**    | Akumulasi penciptaan nilai tambah pasar atas modal ekuitas yang disetorkan pemegang saham sejak berdiri.                                                                                     |
| **Market Value Added (MVA) - Total Entity**               | $\text{MVA} = (\text{Market Value of Stock} + \text{Market Value of Debt}) - \text{Total Investor-Supplied Capital}$ | **Hal. 81, 1085**    | Penciptaan nilai tambah pasar dari seluruh modal entitas perusahaan.                                                                                                                         |
| **Economic Value Added (EVA)**                            | $\text{EVA} = \text{NOPAT} - (\text{Total Net Operating Capital} \times \text{WACC})$<br>$= \text{Total Net Operating Capital} \times (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC})$ | **Hal. 80, 1086**    | Laba ekonomi riil per tahun setelah memperhitungkan seluruh biaya peluang atas modal operasi (cost of capital).                                                                              |

# **Bab 3: Analysis of Financial Statements**

**Cakupan Buku: Halaman 101–136 (Selected Eq: Hal. 1086–1087)**

| **Nama Rumus / Konsep**               | **Rumus Matematis**                                                                                                                                                               | **Halaman Buku**       | **Keterangan & Aplikasi**                                                                 |
|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Current Ratio**                     | $\text{Current Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$ | **Hal. 104, 1086**     | Mengukur kemampuan aset likuid jangka pendek menutupi kewajiban lancar.                   |
| **Quick Ratio (Acid Test)**           | $\text{Quick Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets} - \text{Inventories}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$ | **Hal. 105, 1086**     | Likuiditas tanpa memperhitungkan persediaan yang membutuhkan waktu untuk dicairkan.       |
| **Inventory Turnover Ratio**          | $\text{Inventory Turnover} = \frac{\text{Cost of Goods Sold}}{\text{Inventories}}$ (atau $\frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Inventories}}$) | **Hal. 106, 1086**     | Frekuensi perputaran persediaan barang dagang dalam siklus operasi normal.                |
| **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**      | $\text{DSO} = \frac{\text{Receivables}}{\text{Average Sales Per Day}} = \frac{\text{Receivables}}{\text{Annual Sales} / 365}$ | **Hal. 107, 1086**     | Rata-rata hari yang diperlukan perusahaan untuk mengonversi penjualan kredit menjadi kas. |
| **Fixed Assets Turnover Ratio**       | $\text{Fixed Assets Turnover} = \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Net Fixed Assets}}$ | **Hal. 108, 1086**     | Produktivitas pemanfaatan aktiva tetap berwujud dalam mencetak penjualan.                 |
| **Total Assets Turnover Ratio**       | $\text{Total Assets Turnover} = \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ | **Hal. 108, 1086**     | Efisiensi penggunaan seluruh sumber daya aktiva dalam menghasilkan pendapatan.            |
| **Debt-to-Assets Ratio (Debt Ratio)** | $\text{Debt-to-Assets Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Assets}} = \frac{\text{Short-Term Debt} + \text{Long-Term Debt}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ | **Hal. 110, 1086**     | Proporsi pendanaan aktiva perusahaan yang bersumber dari utang berbunga.                  |
| **Liabilities-to-Assets Ratio**       | $\text{Liabilities-to-Assets Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Liabilities}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ | **Hal. 111, 1086**     | Persentase aset yang dijamin dengan seluruh kewajiban lancar dan jangka panjang.          |
| **Market Debt Ratio**                 | $\text{Market Debt Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Debt} + \text{Market Value of Equity}}$ | **Hal. 111, 1086**     | Struktur modal berbasis nilai pasar utang dan nilai kapitalisasi pasar ekuitas.           |
| **Debt-to-Equity Ratio (D/E)**        | $\text{Debt-to-Equity Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Common Equity}}$ | **Hal. 111, 1086**     | Rasio perbandingan leverage keuangan antara modal utang berbunga terhadap modal saham.    |
| **Equity Multiplier**                 | $\text{Equity Multiplier} = \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Total Common Equity}}$ | **Hal. 112, 1086**     | Pengali pengungkit aset atas ekuitas pemegang saham (komponen pendorong leverage DuPont). |
| **Times-Interest-Earned (TIE) Ratio** | $\text{TIE} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Interest Charges}}$ | **Hal. 112, 1086**     | Kemampuan laba operasi dalam membayar beban bunga periodik.                               |
| **EBITDA Coverage Ratio**             | $\text{EBITDA Coverage} = \frac{\text{EBITDA} + \text{Lease Payments}}{\text{Interest} + \text{Principal Payments} + \text{Lease Payments}}$ | **Hal. 113, 1086**     | Kapasitas arus kas operasi bruto dalam melunasi seluruh kewajiban pembiayaan tetap.       |
| **Net Profit Margin**                 | $\text{Net Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Net Income Available to Common Stockholders}}{\text{Sales}}$ | **Hal. 114, 1086**     | Margin laba bersih akhir yang tersisa untuk pemilik saham dari setiap unit penjualan.     |
| **Operating Profit Margin**           | $\text{Operating Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Sales}}$ | **Hal. 114, 1086**     | Margin laba usaha operasional murni sebelum biaya pendanaan modal.                        |
| **Gross Profit Margin**               | $\text{Gross Profit Margin} = \frac{\text{Sales} - \text{Cost of Goods Sold}}{\text{Sales}}$ | **Hal. 115, 1086**     | Margin keuntungan bruto atas harga pokok penjualan.                                       |
| **Return on Total Assets (ROA)**      | $\text{ROA} = \frac{\text{Net Income Available to Common Stockholders}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ | **Hal. 115, 1086**     | Efektivitas pengembalian laba bersih atas total investasi aset.                           |
| **Basic Earning Power (BEP) Ratio**   | $\text{BEP} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ | **Hal. 115, 1087**     | Kemampuan aset murni menghasilkan laba operasi terlepas dari beban bunga dan pajak.       |
| **DuPont ROA Formulation**            | $\text{ROA} = \text{Profit Margin} \times \text{Total Assets Turnover} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Sales}} \times \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ | **Hal. 123, 1087**     | Korelasi pembentuk ROA melalui profitabilitas penjualan dan perputaran aktiva.            |
| **Return on Common Equity (ROE)**     | $\text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net Income Available to Common Stockholders}}{\text{Common Equity}}$ | **Hal. 116, 1087**     | Tingkat imbal hasil laba bersih atas modal ekuitas biasa.                                 |
| **Extended DuPont Equation**          | $\text{ROE} = \text{ROA} \times \text{Equity Multiplier} = \text{Profit Margin} \times \text{Total Assets Turnover} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}$<br>$= \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Sales}} \times \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}} \times \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Common Equity}}$ | **Hal. 123, 1087**     | Dekomposisi komprehensif pendorong ROE: margin laba, perputaran aset, dan leverage.       |
| **Price/Earnings (P/E) Ratio**        | $\text{P/E Ratio} = \frac{\text{Price Per Share}}{\text{Earnings Per Share (EPS)}}$ | **Hal. 117, 1087**     | Valuasi pasar atas setiap dolar laba bersih per lembar saham.                             |
| **Price/Cash Flow Ratio**             | $\text{Price/Cash Flow} = \frac{\text{Price Per Share}}{\text{Cash Flow Per Share}}$ | **Hal. 118, 1087**     | Valuasi pasar saham dibandingkan arus kas per lembar saham.                               |
| **Book Value Per Share (BVPS)**       | $\text{BVPS} = \frac{\text{Common Equity}}{\text{Shares Outstanding}}$ | **Hal. 63, 119, 1087** | Nilai buku ekuitas tercatat akuntansi untuk setiap lembar saham.                          |
| **Market-to-Book (M/B) Ratio**        | $\text{M/B Ratio} = \frac{\text{Market Price Per Share}}{\text{Book Value Per Share}} = \frac{\text{Market Cap}}{\text{Common Equity}}$ | **Hal. 119, 1087**     | Perbandingan nilai pasar ekuitas terhadap nilai buku historisnya.                         |

# **Bab 4: Time Value of Money**

**Cakupan Buku: Halaman 139–192 (Selected Eq: Hal. 1087–1088)**

| **Nama Rumus / Konsep**                                   | **Rumus Matematis**                                                                                                     | **Halaman Buku**   | **Keterangan & Aplikasi**                                                                     |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Future Value of a Lump Sum (FV)**                       | $FV_N = PV \times (1 + I)^N$ | **Hal. 142, 1087** | Nilai akumulasi masa depan dari nilai tunai saat ini dengan bunga majemuk I selama N periode. |
| **Present Value of a Lump Sum (PV)**                      | $PV = \frac{FV_N}{(1 + I)^N} = FV_N \times (1 + I)^{-N}$ | **Hal. 149, 1087** | Nilai diskonto saat ini dari arus kas masa depan FV_N.                                        |
| **Present Value of a Perpetuity**                         | $PV = \frac{\text{PMT}}{I}$ | **Hal. 154, 1087** | Nilai tunai dari anuitas abadi yang tidak pernah jatuh tempo.                                 |
| **Future Value of an Ordinary Annuity (FVA)**             | $FVA_N = \text{PMT} \times \left[\frac{(1 + I)^N - 1}{I}\right]$ | **Hal. 156, 1087** | Nilai masa depan akumulasi anuitas dengan pembayaran di setiap akhir periode.                 |
| **Future Value of an Annuity Due**                        | $FVA_{\text{due}} = FVA_{\text{ordinary}} \times (1 + I) = \text{PMT} \times \left[\frac{(1 + I)^N - 1}{I}\right] \times (1 + I)$ | **Hal. 159, 1087** | Nilai masa depan anuitas jika pembayaran disetor di awal periode.                             |
| **Present Value of an Ordinary Annuity (PVA)**            | $PVA_N = \text{PMT} \times \left[\frac{1 - (1 + I)^{-N}}{I}\right] = \text{PMT} \times \left[\frac{1}{I} - \frac{1}{I(1 + I)^N}\right]$ | **Hal. 160, 1087** | Nilai tunai dari seluruh pembayaran periodik yang diterima di akhir setiap periode.           |
| **Present Value of an Annuity Due**                       | $PVA_{\text{due}} = PVA_{\text{ordinary}} \times (1 + I) = \text{PMT} \times \left[\frac{1 - (1 + I)^{-N}}{I}\right] \times (1 + I)$ | **Hal. 161, 1087** | Nilai tunai anuitas jika cicilan diterima/dibayar di awal periode.                            |
| **Present Value of Uneven Cash Flows**                    | $PV_{\text{uneven}} = \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{CF_t}{(1 + I)^t}$ | **Hal. 166, 1087** | Nilai sekarang dari aliran arus kas yang jumlahnya bervariasi setiap periode.                 |
| **Future Value of Uneven Cash Flows**                     | $FV_{\text{uneven}} = \sum_{t=1}^{N} CF_t \times (1 + I)^{N - t}$ | **Hal. 168, 1087** | Nilai masa depan pada tahun ke-N dari serangkaian arus kas tidak beraturan.                   |
| **Periodic Interest Rate (I_PER)**                        | $I_{\text{PER}} = \frac{I_{\text{NOM}}}{M}$ | **Hal. 170, 1088** | Suku bunga per periode pemajemukan majemuk (M = frekuensi compounding per tahun).             |
| **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**                          | $\text{APR} = I_{\text{PER}} \times M = I_{\text{NOM}}$ | **Hal. 171, 1088** | Suku bunga nominal tahunan tanpa memperhitungkan efek pemajemukan antar-periode.              |
| **Effective Annual Rate (EAR / EFF%)**                    | $\text{EAR} = (1 + I_{\text{PER}})^M - 1 = \left(1 + \frac{I_{\text{NOM}}}{M}\right)^M - 1$ | **Hal. 171, 1088** | Tingkat bunga tahunan riil yang memperhitungkan efek pemajemukan M kali setahun.              |
| **Future Value with Fractional / Non-Annual Compounding** | $FV_N = PV \times \left(1 + \frac{I_{\text{NOM}}}{M}\right)^{M \times N}$ | **Hal. 171, 1088** | Perhitungan nilai masa depan dengan frekuensi pemajemukan beberapa kali setahun.              |
| **Growing Annuity (PV & FV)**                             | $PV = \frac{\text{PMT}_1}{I - g} \times \left[1 - \left(\frac{1 + g}{1 + I}\right)^N\right]$<br>atau $FV = \frac{\text{PMT}_1}{I - g} \times \left[(1 + I)^N - (1 + g)^N\right]$ | **Hal. 178–180**   | Nilai anuitas yang nominal pembayarannya bertumbuh dengan laju konstan g setiap periode.      |

| **Total Expected Bond Return Decomposition**     | $\text{Yield to Maturity} = \text{Current Yield} + \text{Capital Gains Yield}$ | **Hal. 206, 1088** | Pemisahan total return obligasi atas porsi pendapatan kupon dan perubahan harga pasar obligasi.                                                  |
| **Nominal Quoted Interest Rate Determination**   | $r_d = r^* + \text{IP} + \text{MRP} + \text{DRP} + \text{LP}$ | **Hal. 213, 1088** | Pembentukan suku bunga pasar: suku bunga riil bebas risiko (r\*), inflasi (IP), premi jatuh tempo (MRP), gagal bayar (DRP), dan likuiditas (LP). |
| **Inflation Premium Formulation (IP_N)**         | $\text{IP}_N = \frac{I_1 + I_2 + \cdots + I_N}{N}$ | **Hal. 214, 1088** | Rata-rata ekspektasi laju inflasi selama periode N tahun masa berlaku obligasi.                                                                  |

# **Bab 6: Risk and Return**

**Cakupan Buku: Halaman 241–292 (Selected Eq: Hal. 1088–1089)**

| **Nama Rumus / Konsep**                               | **Rumus Matematis**                                                                                                     | **Halaman Buku**   | **Keterangan & Aplikasi**                                                                                |
|-------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Expected Rate of Return (Discrete Distribution)**   | $\hat{r} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} P_i \times r_i$ | **Hal. 243, 1088** | Nilai ekspektasi tertimbang imbal hasil berdasarkan probabilitas kejadian skenario P_i.                  |
| **Historical Sample Average Return**                  | $\bar{r}_{\text{Avg}} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{T} \bar{r}_t}{T}$ | **Hal. 249, 1088** | Rata-rata aritmatika pengembalian historis selama T periode observasi.                                   |
| **Variance of Returns (Discrete)**                    | $\sigma^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} (r_i - \hat{r})^2 \times P_i$ | **Hal. 245, 1088** | Ukuran dispersi atau variasi kemungkinan hasil terhadap rata-rata tertimbangnya.                         |
| **Standard Deviation of Returns (σ)**                 | $\sigma = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (r_i - \hat{r})^2 \times P_i}$ | **Hal. 245, 1088** | Ukuran risiko total penyimpangan hasil investasi pada distribusi probabilitas diskret.                   |
| **Historical Sample Standard Deviation (S)**          | $S = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{t=1}^{T} (\bar{r}_t - \bar{r}_{\text{Avg}})^2}{T - 1}}$ | **Hal. 250, 1089** | Standar deviasi sampel pengembalian historis dengan pembagi derajat kebebasan (T-1).                     |
| **Coefficient of Variation (CV)**                     | $\text{CV} = \frac{\sigma}{\hat{r}}$ | **Hal. 246**       | Ukuran risiko relatif per unit imbal hasil ekspektasi.                                                   |
| **Portfolio Expected Return**                         | $\hat{r}_p = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \times \hat{r}_i$ | **Hal. 253, 1089** | Rata-rata tertimbang ekspektasi return masing-masing aset dalam komposisi bobot w_i.                     |
| **Portfolio Standard Deviation (Discrete Scenarios)** | $\sigma_p = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (r_{pi} - \hat{r}_p)^2 \times P_i}$ | **Hal. 254, 1089** | Standar deviasi imbal hasil portofolio pada berbagai kondisi skenario ekonomi.                           |
| **Historical Sample Correlation Coefficient (ρ / R)** | $R = \frac{\sum (\bar{r}_{it} - \bar{r}_{i,\text{Avg}})(\bar{r}_{jt} - \bar{r}_{j,\text{Avg}})}{\sqrt{\sum (\bar{r}_{it} - \bar{r}_{i,\text{Avg}})^2 \times \sum (\bar{r}_{jt} - \bar{r}_{j,\text{Avg}})^2}}$ | **Hal. 255, 1089** | Koefisien korelasi kovarians terstandardisasi antara aset i dan j (-1 hingga +1).                        |
| **Covariance with Market Portfolio (COV\_{iM})**      | $\text{COV}_{iM} = \rho_{iM} \times \sigma_i \times \sigma_M$ | **Hal. 258, 1089** | Kovariansi antara pergerakan return saham i dengan return indeks pasar saham.                            |

# **Bab 21: Dynamic Capital Structures and Corporate Valuation**

**Cakupan Buku: Halaman 849–872 (Selected Eq: Hal. 1095–1096)**

| **Nama Rumus / Konsep**                               | **Rumus Matematis**                                                                                                                                 | **Halaman Buku**   | **Keterangan & Aplikasi**                                                                                                  |
|-------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **General Adjusted Present Value (APV)**              | $V_L = V_U + V_{\text{Tax Shield}}$ | **Hal. 851, 1095** | Nilai perusahaan berutang sama dengan nilai entitas tanpa utang ditambah nilai tunai perlindungan pajak bunga.             |
| **Tax Shield Valuation (General Dynamic APV)**        | $V_{\text{Tax Shield}} = \frac{r_d \times T \times D}{r_{\text{TS}} - g_L} \;\Rightarrow\; V_L = V_U + \frac{r_d}{r_{\text{TS}} - g_L} \times T \times D$ | **Hal. 854, 1095** | Valuasi diskonto tax shield pada tingkat diskonto khusus risiko penghematan pajak r\_{TS}.                                 |
| **Compressed Adjusted Present Value (CAPV)**          | $V_L = V_U + \frac{r_d \times T \times D}{r_{sU} - g_L}$ | **Hal. 855, 1096** | Model CAPV di mana risiko perlindungan pajak diasumsikan setara dengan risiko operasional tanpa utang (r\_{TS} = r\_{sU}). |
| **Cost of Equity in CAPV Model**                      | $r_{sL} = r_{sU} + (r_{sU} - r_d) \times \frac{D}{S}$ | **Hal. 855, 1096** | Biaya modal saham berutang dalam kerangka Compressed APV.                                                                  |
| **Beta Adjustment in CAPV Model**                     | $b = b_U + (b_U - b_D) \times \frac{D}{S}$ | **Hal. 855, 1096** | Hubungan beta ekuitas dengan memperhitungkan potensi risiko pada beta utang (b_D).                                         |
| **Unlevered Cost of Equity (r\_{sU})**                | $r_{sU} = w_s \times r_{sL} + w_d \times r_d$ | **Hal. 856, 1096** | Biaya modal rata-rata aset perusahaan tanpa fasilitas pengurangan pajak bunga.                                             |
| **Annual Tax Savings from Interest**                  | $\text{Tax Savings} = \text{Interest Expense} \times \text{Tax Rate} = r_d \times D \times T$ | **Hal. 857, 1096** | Penghematan arus kas keluar pajak tahunan yang tercipta dari pemanfaatan utang berbunga.                                   |
| **Horizon Value of Unlevered Firm (HV\_{U,N})**       | $HV_{U,N} = \frac{\text{FCF}_{N+1}}{r_{sU} - g_L} = \frac{\text{FCF}_N \times (1 + g_L)}{r_{sU} - g_L}$ | **Hal. 857, 1096** | Nilai horizon perusahaan tanpa utang pada akhir masa estimasi bertingkat eksplisit.                                        |
| **Horizon Value of Tax Shield (HV\_{TS,N})**          | $HV_{\text{TS},N} = \frac{\text{TS}_{N+1}}{r_{sU} - g_L} = \frac{\text{TS}_N \times (1 + g_L)}{r_{sU} - g_L}$ | **Hal. 858, 1096** | Nilai horizon dari kesinambungan penghematan pajak bunga di masa depan.                                                    |
| **Total Value of Operations via APV**                 | $V_{\text{op}} = V_{\text{Unlevered}} + V_{\text{Tax Shield}} = \left[\sum \frac{\text{FCF}_t}{(1 + r_{sU})^t} + \frac{HV_U}{(1 + r_{sU})^N}\right] + \left[\sum \frac{\text{TS}_t}{(1 + r_{sU})^t} + \frac{HV_{\text{TS}}}{(1 + r_{sU})^N}\right]$ | **Hal. 858, 1096** | Perhitungan menyeluruh nilai operasi pada perusahaan dengan struktur utang dinamis.                                        |
| **Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) - Cash Flow View**  | $\text{FCFE} = \text{FCF} - \text{Interest Expense} + \text{Interest Tax Shield} + \text{Net Change in Debt}$ | **Hal. 863, 1096** | Arus kas yang tersedia murni hanya untuk dibagikan kepada pemegang saham biasa.                                            |
| **Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) - Net Income View** | $\text{FCFE} = \text{Net Income} - \text{Net Investment in Operating Capital} + \text{Net Change in Debt}$ | **Hal. 864, 1096** | Formulasi arus kas ekuitas langsung berbasis laba bersih laporan laba rugi.                                                |
| **Horizon Value of Equity via FCFE (HV\_{FCFE,N})**   | $HV_{\text{FCFE},N} = \frac{\text{FCFE}_{N+1}}{r_{sL} - g_L} = \frac{\text{FCFE}_N \times (1 + g_L)}{r_{sL} - g_L}$ | **Hal. 864, 1096** | Nilai terminal ekuitas pada model diskonto FCFE.                                                                           |
| **Total Equity Value via FCFE Valuation**             | $S = \sum_{t=1}^{N} \frac{\text{FCFE}_t}{(1 + r_{sL})^t} + \frac{HV_{\text{FCFE},N}}{(1 + r_{sL})^N} + \text{Nonoperating Assets}$ | **Hal. 865, 1096** | Penetapan langsung nilai ekuitas pemegang saham tanpa mendiskontokan FCF entitas total.                                    |

# **Bab 25: Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing Models**

**Cakupan Buku: Halaman 981–1014 (Selected Eq: Hal. 1096–1097)**

| **Nama Rumus / Konsep**                                  | **Rumus Matematis**                                                                                                                          | **Halaman Buku**    | **Keterangan & Aplikasi**                                                                                |
|----------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Two-Asset Portfolio Expected Return**                  | $\hat{r}_p = w_A \times \hat{r}_A + (1 - w_A) \times \hat{r}_B$ | **Hal. 983, 1096**  | Ekspektasi pengembalian portofolio kombinasi dua instrumen aset berisiko A dan B.                        |
| **Two-Asset Portfolio Standard Deviation**               | $\sigma_p = \sqrt{w_A^2 \times \sigma_A^2 + (1 - w_A)^2 \times \sigma_B^2 + 2 \times w_A \times (1 - w_A) \times \rho_{AB} \times \sigma_A \times \sigma_B}$ | **Hal. 984, 1096**  | Volatilitas total risiko portofolio dua aset dengan mempertimbangkan korelasi kovarians antar aset.      |

| **N-Asset Portfolio Variance (Double Summation)**        | $\sigma_p^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{j=1}^{N} w_i \times w_j \times \sigma_i \times \sigma_j \times \rho_{ij} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i^2 \times \sigma_i^2 + \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{j \neq i} w_i \times w_j \times \text{Cov}_{ij}$ | **Hal. 988, 1097**  | Formula matriks varians-kovariansi risiko portofolio multi-aset.                                         |

| **Capital Market Line (CML)**                            | $\hat{r}_p = r_{\text{RF}} + \frac{\hat{r}_M - r_{\text{RF}}}{\sigma_M} \times \sigma_p$ | **Hal. 992, 1097**  | Garis pasar modal yang menghubungkan imbal hasil ekspektasi dan risiko total σ untuk portofolio efisien. |
| **Security Market Line (SML) Derivation**                | $r_i = r_{\text{RF}} + \frac{r_M - r_{\text{RF}}}{\sigma_M^2} \times \text{Cov}(r_i, r_M) = r_{\text{RF}} + (r_M - r_{\text{RF}}) \times b_i$ | **Hal. 994, 1097**  | Garis pasar sekuritas yang mendasarkan premi risiko aset hanya pada risiko kovarians sistematisnya.      |
| **Beta of Asset i via Covariance**                       | $b_i = \frac{\text{Cov}(r_i, r_M)}{\sigma_M^2} = \frac{\rho_{iM} \times \sigma_i \times \sigma_M}{\sigma_M^2} = \rho_{iM} \times \frac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_M}$ | **Hal. 995, 1097**  | Rasio kovariansi aset terhadap varians pasar yang merupakan slope regresi imbal hasil pasar.             |
| **Sharpe Reward-to-Variability Ratio**                   | $\text{Sharpe Ratio} = \frac{\bar{r}_p - \bar{r}_{\text{RF}}}{\sigma_p}$ | **Hal. 1004, 1097** | Kinerja portofolio berbasis kelebihan return per unit deviasi standar risiko total.                      |
| **Treynor Reward-to-Volatility Ratio**                   | $\text{Treynor Ratio} = \frac{\bar{r}_p - \bar{r}_{\text{RF}}}{b_p}$ | **Hal. 1004, 1097** | Kinerja portofolio berbasis kelebihan return per unit risiko sistematis beta.                            |
| **Total Risk Decomposition (Index Model)**               | $\sigma_i^2 = b_i^2 \times \sigma_M^2 + \sigma_{e,i}^2$ (Market Risk + Firm-Specific Unique Risk) | **Hal. 999, 1097**  | Pemisahan varians total saham menjadi komponen risiko pasar sistematis dan risiko idiosinkratik unik.    |
